Privacy Policy

Application: Bubble Level (package com.kont.bubblelevel)
Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Bubble Level application handles. It is written to match the actual behaviour of the app, not a generic template.

1. Data the developer collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers and no analytics belonging to the developer. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores a few small values locally, in the app's own private storage:

  • your calibration offsets (a few decimal numbers),
  • whether the level sound is on or off,
  • usage counters that decide when to show the Google Play rating prompt (the number of separate days the app was used, total time of use, and whether the prompt has already been shown).

The developer cannot see any of these. The sound setting is included in Android's own backup and device-to-device transfer (if Google backup is turned on, Android may back it up to your Google account). Calibration offsets are deliberately excluded from backup and transfer, because they belong to this phone's sensor; on a new phone you simply calibrate again. The rating-prompt counters are not backed up either. The values on your device are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings.

3. Sensors

The app reads your device's accelerometer / gravity sensor to calculate tilt. On Android, motion sensors do not require a runtime permission. Sensor readings are used only to draw the level on screen and are never stored or transmitted.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size),
  • ad interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked).

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller/processor under its own terms. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a Google-certified consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time it runs. You can accept or refuse personalised advertising there. You can change your choice later with Ad privacy choices below the buttons on the main screen; this option appears only in regions where consent is required. If consent is not given, the app still works in full; only the advertising behaviour changes.

Controlling advertising on your device

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by manufacturer).

5. Permissions

The app itself requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, photos, files, SMS or call history.

The following permissions are declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and are visible in the app's manifest: internet access, network state, advertising ID and Android Ad Services permissions and wake lock (declared by the SDK's internal scheduling library). The level measurement itself does not use the internet.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Data deletion

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request deletion of. To remove everything the app stored on your device, uninstall the app or use Settings → Apps → Bubble Level → Storage → Clear data.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: konttechnology@gmail.com

  • cihaz bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, ekran boyutu),
  • reklam etkileşim verileri (reklamın gösterilmesi, görüntülenmesi, tıklanması).

Bu işleme Google tarafından kendi koşulları kapsamında yapılır. Ayrıntılar: Google'ın hizmetlerini kullanan uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı ve Google Gizlilik Politikası.
